Welcome,

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Political Science at Rutgers University. My research examines how contemporary geopolitical and environmental risks reshape strategic behavior in international politics, with a focus on territorial disputes, energy/climate politics, and corporate political activity.

Methodologically, I combine computational text analysis, survey experiments, and quantitative models to capture how citizens, states, and firms adapt to shifting distributions of power, risk, and regulation. My research has previously appeared in Economics & Politics and Turkish Studies.
